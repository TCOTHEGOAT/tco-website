✦   BEHIND THE NAME

WHO IS
TCO?

Before the music, there was a kid trying to figure out where he belonged. Then he found a stage, found his voice, and decided to become the person he always felt called to be.

01 / MY STORY

IF NOT ME, THEN WHO?

I was always a talkative person, even though I was a shy kid. I was never the coolest kid in the room, but I knew I was smart, and I always had a lot of love to give. I sang on the bus to school. I joined drama clubs and choirs. I played Yu-Gi-Oh in the cafeteria with my friends. Maybe I was a little bit of a nerd. I never had trouble making friends or finding people who supported me, but I had a harder time finding myself.

I spent a lot of my younger life trying to understand where I fit. I never felt black enough for the black kids or white enough for the white kids. Before music, I carried an identity crisis that I didn't always know how to explain.

Then I found the stage. Acting, singing, performing, it never really mattered what the stage was. When I was in front of people, something clicked. It felt like home. It was one of the few places where I felt truly seen and loved. About five years ago, I made my first song. I started as a rapper, but the moment I heard my own voice, I knew this could be bigger than me. I knew I had something to say, and I knew music was my path.

R&B became the place where I could finally be completely free. I could love hard. I could be vulnerable. I could make the kind of music people sometimes joke about as “simp music” and mean every word of it. I make music for people who feel deeply, for people who are hurting, and for men who were taught they always have to be okay. I want men to know that being vulnerable doesn't make you weak. You can love somebody with your whole heart and still be a man. You can hurt and still keep going. You can lose love and never stop believing in it.

That belief is personal. I lost my mother when I was a baby, and my father was never really a father to me. I grew up carrying that absence, along with bullying and the feeling that I had lost so much love before I ever understood what love was. For a long time, I looked for that unconditional love in the wrong places. I chose women who didn't respect me, and I kept searching for something I couldn't name. Looking back, sometimes I wonder if I was trying to find my mother inside the women I loved.

Life has a strange sense of irony. I met the mother of my daughter at the karaoke bar where I sing. We fell in love, moved in together, and started a family. Then everything changed. She left, got married, had another child, and I was left dealing with child support and not being able to see my daughter as often as I wanted. I was hurt for a long time. I stopped believing in love. I felt broken to my core.

But I got back up.

Becoming a father changed me in a way nothing else could. I stopped living only for myself. It opened my mind to the world and made me want something better, not just for my daughter, but for every child who deserves to grow up surrounded by love. Fatherhood taught me that no matter how heavy life gets, you keep pushing forward because somebody always needs you.

And somehow, after everything, I fell in love again. That story isn't finished. Maybe that's for the next album.

THE NAME

THE CHOSEN ONE

I have always felt called to be more and do more. I love God, and I believe His strongest soldiers are often tested the most. Through loss, rejection, bullying, heartbreak, and everything that tried to make me smaller, I kept loving harder and trying to become better. I knew something was special about me. I knew I was the one.

THE SOUND

LOVE WITHOUT APOLOGY

Love is who we are. What we love means everything, and who we love can reveal how we love ourselves. My R&B is built for people who feel everything. I want my music to remind men that they are allowed to hurt, allowed to love hard, and allowed to say they are not okay.

THE MISSION

BE A LIGHT

I want it all. I want to become one of the biggest artists in the world, but the goal is bigger than fame. I want TCO to be a voice for the voiceless, a beacon for love and hope, and a light in a world that can feel hateful. I want someone listening to know there is still love in this world, and they are not alone.

THE PROMISE

KEEP LOVING.

Even after everything I've been through, I don't want to become numb. I want to keep loving. I want to keep creating. I want to keep moving forward. And I'm okay walking alone if that's what the journey requires.
